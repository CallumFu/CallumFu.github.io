Biography

I am a researcher working at the intersection of energy systems, optimization, and climate resilience. My work develops data-driven models and decision-support tools for reliable and sustainable infrastructure under uncertainty.

My research aims to bridge engineering systems and real-world decision making, with a focus on resilience analysis, risk-aware planning, and scalable optimization for complex networks.

I collaborate with academic and industry partners on methods that connect theory, computation, and practice. This site collects selected publications, talks, awards, and recent updates.
